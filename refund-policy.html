Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Deposits

A 50% deposit is required to confirm every order. This deposit reserves your pickup slot and covers the cost of fresh ingredients purchased specifically for your order.

Cancellations

Because all fruit is candied and prepared fresh on your pickup day, cancellations made at least 48 hours before your pickup date are eligible for a full deposit refund. Cancellations made less than 48 hours before pickup are non-refundable, as ingredients have already been purchased and/or prep has begun.

Missed Pickups / No-Shows

If you do not pick up your order on the scheduled date and do not contact Kait to reschedule, the deposit will be forfeited and the remaining balance may still be due.

Pickup Window

Standard pickup cutoff is 8:00 PM on your scheduled pickup day. Orders not picked up by then are treated as a missed pickup under the policy above. Need a later pickup time? Message Kait in advance to request one — a $15 late pickup fee applies for approved after-hours pickups, and it's not guaranteed depending on availability.

If Kait Needs to Cancel

On the rare occasion Kait is unable to fulfill your order (illness, ingredient shortage, weather, etc.), you will be notified as soon as possible and receive a full refund of your deposit, no questions asked.

Substitutions

If a specific fruit or ingredient is unavailable on your prep day, Kait will contact you when possible to offer a similar substitution or adjust your total for that item. If you can't be reached in time, a reasonable substitution may be made at Kait's discretion, or that portion of your order will be refunded.

Storage & Consume-By Guidance

These are fresh-prepared frozen treats, not shelf-stable candy — keep them frozen until you're ready to eat, and for best taste and texture, we recommend enjoying candied fruit items within about 5–7 days of pickup. Quality (texture, flavor) will decline the longer they sit in a home freezer, and any quality claim outside this window is unlikely to be eligible for a refund.

Packaged candy toppings/inclusions (like the Sour Gushers) are manufactured products with their own printed shelf life — check the label on the original package for that item's best-by date, since it may last longer than the fresh fruit portion of your order.

Allergens & Cross-Contact

Orders are prepared in a home kitchen that also handles common allergens (including but not limited to tree nuts, dairy, and gluten). While we take care during prep, we cannot guarantee any item is free of cross-contact with allergens. If you have a food allergy, please note it in your order's special requests — but understand that Kait cannot fully eliminate cross-contact risk, and orders are not eligible for refund based on allergen sensitivity that was not disclosed at the time of ordering.

Payment Method Finality

Deposits and payments are sent via Venmo, Zelle, or Apple Cash. These are peer-to-peer payment methods with no built-in buyer protection — once sent, payments are final on the platform side. If a refund is approved under this policy, it will be issued back through the same payment method used.

Pickup Only

Kait's Fruit Pop Shop is local pickup only in Liberty, TX and surrounding areas — no shipping or delivery is offered. Refund requests related to delivery, shipping delays, or transit issues do not apply.

Order Changes

Need to adjust your order (fruit selection, toppings, or quantity)? Message Kait as soon as possible. Changes are not guaranteed, even if requested more than 48 hours before pickup — they depend on ingredient availability and prep schedule at the time of your request. Changes requested within 48 hours of pickup are unlikely to be accommodated, as ingredients are typically already purchased and prep may have begun.

Quality Issues

All quality-issue claims (incorrect items, spoiled or off product, packaging problems, etc.) must include a clear photo of the item(s) in question, sent within 24 hours of pickup. Claims without a photo, or reported after 24 hours, will not be eligible for a refund or replacement.

Refunds or replacements for verified quality issues are issued at Kait's discretion and are typically partial, reflecting the affected portion of the order rather than the full order total.

Non-Refundable Situations

  • Change of mind after the order has been prepared
  • Cancellations made less than 48 hours before pickup
  • No-shows without prior notice
  • Quality complaints made without a photo, or made more than 24 hours after pickup
  • Personal taste preferences (e.g., "too sour," "too sweet") when the order matches what was ordered
  • Order change requests that could not be accommodated due to timing or ingredient availability

Questions?

Reach out to Kait directly via the contact info on the order page — we're happy to help.
