Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Deposits

A 50% deposit is required to confirm every order. This deposit reserves your pickup slot and covers the cost of fresh ingredients purchased specifically for your order.

Cancellations

Because all fruit is candied and prepared fresh on your pickup day, cancellations made at least 48 hours before your pickup date are eligible for a full deposit refund. Cancellations made less than 48 hours before pickup are non-refundable, as ingredients have already been purchased and/or prep has begun.

Missed Pickups / No-Shows

If you do not pick up your order on the scheduled date and do not contact Kait to reschedule, the deposit will be forfeited and the remaining balance may still be due.

Order Changes

Need to adjust your order (fruit selection, toppings, or quantity)? Message Kait as soon as possible. Changes can only be accommodated before prep begins for your pickup day.

Quality Issues

If there is a problem with your order (incorrect items, quality concern, etc.), please contact Kait within 24 hours of pickup with details and, if possible, a photo. We'll make it right with a replacement or partial refund, evaluated case-by-case.

Non-Refundable Situations

  • Change of mind after the order has been prepared
  • Cancellations made less than 48 hours before pickup
  • No-shows without prior notice

Questions?

Reach out to Kait directly via the contact info on the order page — we're happy to help.
